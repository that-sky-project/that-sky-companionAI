Sky terrain resource readers — provenance and licenses

The production readers are new bounded implementations informed by the MIT
SkyVEx mesh_parser.py and MIT SkyBstbake layouts, pinned via SkyVEx commit
b38609d2367a23d667514e9ef24947c43f987b97. Byte-layout evidence and native-fixture
validation are kept separately in research/formats. These notices are retained
for the referenced MIT implementations.

The optional compressed-vertex adapter invokes the separately installed official
meshoptimizer 1.3.0 JavaScript/WASM decoder (MIT). Its distribution and license
remain under engine/node_modules/meshoptimizer. No automatic install occurs.
https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer

The LGPL-2.1 meshes2obj_json.py reference was used only to produce offline test
oracle arrays. It is not imported, copied, bundled, or executed by production.
Old SkyEngineTools (ISC) was a historical layout comparison only; its hard-coded
positions layout is not used as a current-format fallback.
